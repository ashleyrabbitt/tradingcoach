01 / Understand the offer

## Know when it starts.  
Know what you’re buying.

TradingView’s November 9 partner announcement describes discounts of up to 80% on eligible annual plans, with one additional month. Details were checked November 10, 2026.

### Two phases. One campaign.

Black Friday
November 23–27, 2026

Cyber Monday
November 28–December 1, 2026

Each phase starts at 12:00 a.m. and ends at 11:59:59 p.m. Pacific Standard Time (UTC−8). The overall campaign ends December 1 at 11:59:59 p.m. PST.

Use the official promotion page to confirm availability. Being within the announced dates does not verify a checkout price.

### Check your eligibility at checkout

- Choose an eligible annual subscription. Do not assume monthly billing receives this offer.
- Sign in to check new subscription, upgrade, extension or renewal eligibility for your account.
- Confirm your plan’s actual discount and that the extra month appears in your subscription term.
- Review currency, taxes, renewal price, cancellation terms and any separate market-data costs.

The announcement does not establish a plan-by-plan price or guarantee eligibility for every account, country or billing method.
